# How to Play

## Starting a run

1. Choose **New Game** on the main menu. First you reach the **rule setup page**: all 19 run rules (difficulty, reincarnation terms, etc.) are editable, defaulting to the recommended values. Once confirmed, the rules are locked — in-game they are read-only.
2. **Birth draw**: name, origin, fate, spirit root and talent each draw 3 candidates; for each field you either keep the draw or pick your own.
3. **Reincarnation-point allocation** (from the second life on): spend cross-life points on Combat / Spirit / Fortune / Physique, or buy birth boons.
4. Your first life begins: a mortal with a crippled spirit root, 50 spirit stones, and sealed past-life memories.

## Living a life

Time flows by month and every action costs time. Age and lifespan (120 years) settle monthly — watch for the "lifespan ending" warning.

- **Cultivate** (12 months): gain cultivation.
- **Break through**: once cultivation meets the current stage threshold, attempt the next minor stage or realm (Qi Refining → Foundation → Golden Core → Nascent Soul → Spirit Transformation → Void Refinement → Union → Mahayana → Tribulation; four stages each). The check rolls d100; failure carries no penalty and builds protection (each failure lowers the next difficulty by 5, up to −20).
- **Rest** (1 month): restore 60% health plus a little cultivation.
- **Explore**: travel between 12 locations (1–6 months by distance). Exploration triggers random events (binary choices, three-way choices, moral forks, etc., rolled on the d80 exploration die); success can drop technique pages, ores or treasures. Alternatively, walk a **five-layer forked route**, choosing your path through combat / elite / event / shop / rest nodes.
- **Commissions & timed posts**: the commission board offers accept-based side quests (max 3 held, no time limit). Timed posts carry dual countdowns — expiry and timeouts settle reputation, so don't over-accept.
- **Sign-ins**: first arrival at the Market, the Qingyun Sect Gate and the Qingming Secret Realm grants a one-time sign-in reward, recorded in a cross-life codex.

## Cultivation & management

- **Professions**: alchemy, forging, spirit farming and fishing each have their own proficiency that grows with use; at 5 / 15 / 30 uses they break through to Skilled / Master / Grandmaster with output bonuses.
- **Forging**: buy ore at the market ore shop (or pick ore up while exploring) and forge treasures at the forge station — gated by both crafting level and realm; success produces one of the 28 treasures.
- **Sect**: join a sect, run errands for contribution, and rise through ranks (promotions grant treasures).
- **Techniques**: learn and comprehend techniques to raise combat power; a technique proficiency system is on the roadmap.
- **Combat**: challenge local opponents on the d20 combat die; treasures, techniques and realm all fold into your strength.
- **Economy**: buy pills at the market, consign pills for spirit stones, build a spirit-gathering array at your cave dwelling. Note: spirit stones are wiped on death — don't hoard.
- **Social**: 12 NPCs can be befriended and visited, developing into five relationship types — ally, master, disciple, dao companion, enemy. Favor feeds social checks.

## Death & reincarnation

Dying of old age, falling in battle, a violent death, or severe qi deviation ends the life and opens **reincarnation settlement**:

- Reincarnation points are granted by the life's grade (S 6 / A 4 / B 3 / C 2), plus tiers for memory shards newly collected this life.
- Only one tenth of cultivation carries over. Memory is retained by manner of death (old age 30% / battle 10% / violent death 0%, with a realm bonus capped at 50%).
- Spend legacy points to redeem equipment and techniques you want to keep; pick one natal treasure — actually held this life and at its realm threshold — to carry over.
- Treasures you buried and karma you left unsettled wait for your next life.

The Reincarnation History page shows your reincarnation tree, past-life fragments and the chronicle of every life.

## Main storyline: The Past-Life Mystery

Collect 9 memory shards. At 1 / 3 / 5 / 7 / 9 shards, five chapters of your past-life identity are revealed; completing the set grants extra rewards and a past-life dao fruit to carry each life.

## Settings

- Three theme modes (light / dark / follow system), switched by the floating button at bottom-right.
- Saves export/import as JSON (format-compatible with the open-source edition — progress migrates both ways).
- AI connection settings live on the settings page (optional): enter your own API key or connect a local model; keys are stored only in your browser. The AI companion panel is on the roadmap; the game is fully playable without it.
