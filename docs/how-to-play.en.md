# How to Play

## Starting a run

1. Choose **New Game** on the main menu. First you reach the **rule setup page**: all 19 run rules (difficulty, reincarnation terms, etc.) are editable, defaulting to the recommended values. Once confirmed, the rules are locked — in-game they are read-only.
2. **Birth draw**: name, origin, fate, spirit root and talent each draw 3 candidates; for each field you either keep the draw or pick your own.
3. **Reincarnation-point allocation** (from the second life on): spend cross-life points on Combat / Spirit / Fortune / Physique, or buy birth boons.
4. Your first life begins: a mortal with a crippled spirit root, 50 spirit stones, and sealed past-life memories.

## Living a life

Time flows by month and every action costs time. Age and lifespan (120 years) settle monthly — watch for the "lifespan ending" warning. There is also a **yearly action-point budget**: 40 points per year, with per-action costs (cultivate 20, explore 10, breakthrough 15, craft 12, sect errand 12, duel 6, social 6, travel 4) and resting always free. When the budget runs out, only free actions remain for that year; it refreshes each new year — plan the year's rhythm ahead.

- **Cultivate** (12 months): gain cultivation.
- **Break through**: once cultivation meets the current stage threshold, attempt the next minor stage or realm (Qi Refining → Foundation → Golden Core → Nascent Soul → Spirit Transformation → Void Refinement → Union → Mahayana → Tribulation; four stages each). The check rolls d100; failure carries no penalty and builds protection (each failure lowers the next difficulty by 5, up to −20). **Major-realm breakthroughs** face two extra gates: you must hold a **Breakthrough Pill** (without one the attempt is blocked, with directions to get one; the pill is consumed only when the check succeeds, never on failure), and a passed check enters a **tribulation** — an omen card, a duel against the tribulation avatar, then settlement. Win and the realm truly rises; lose and you return to Perfection of the current realm, pill kept, cultivation refunded, and the tribulation duel cannot kill you. Pills can be bought at the market or dropped in secret realms — or won at Perfection from the **Forbidden-Ground Duel** on the cultivation page, challenging the guardian boss of your target realm (costs one duel; retry freely after a loss).
- **Rest** (1 month): restore 60% health plus a little cultivation.
- **Explore**: travel between 15 locations (1–6 months by distance). Exploration triggers random events (binary choices, three-way choices, moral forks, etc., rolled on the d80 exploration die; outcomes are tagged by type — encounter / combat / gathering / lore); success can drop technique pages, ores or treasures. Alternatively, walk a **five-layer forked route**, choosing your path through combat / elite / event / shop / rest nodes. Two more ways to explore without travelling: the three **random-exploration modes** in the map tab's Random Exploration zone — Expedition (spirit-stone leaning), Delving (material leaning), Trial Tower (cultivation leaning), each entry counting as one exploration action; and **flip-grid exploration** — open a 3×3 face-down board (3 months + 10 action points) of combat / fortune / rest / hunt / breakthrough cells, settled one flip at a time.
- **Round goals**: each round, pick 1 of 3 candidate mini-goals (explore 3 times, reach 2 locations, …); completing it pays out at once, and expired goals are replaced automatically.
- **Annual events**: once a year a grand event strikes at random (a beast tide, a sect tournament, …). You cannot dodge it — only choose how to meet it.
- **Commissions & timed posts**: the commission board offers accept-based side quests (max 3 held, no time limit), including **quest chains** whose stages unlock in sequence under a whole-chain monthly deadline, with a bonus prize for finishing a chain. Timed posts carry dual countdowns — expiry and timeouts settle reputation, so don't over-accept.
- **Sign-ins**: first arrival at the Market, the Qingyun Sect Gate and the Qingming Secret Realm grants a one-time sign-in reward, recorded in a cross-life codex.

## Cultivation & management

- **Professions**: alchemy, forging, spirit farming and fishing each have their own proficiency that grows with use; at 5 / 15 / 30 uses they break through to Skilled / Master / Grandmaster with output bonuses.
- **Forging**: buy ore at the market ore shop (or pick ore up while exploring) and forge treasures at the forge station — gated by both crafting level and realm; success produces one of the 28 treasures.
- **Sect**: join a sect, run errands for contribution, and rise through ranks (promotions grant treasures).
- **Techniques**: techniques come in five tiers （凡 / 黄 / 玄 / 地 / 天） and grow in layers — cultivating and dueling earn layer points, and a full stack means mastery with a stronger bonus. Every technique you learn is recorded permanently in the knowledge codex, and at settlement you may carry one codex-mastered technique into the next life for free. The rootless can only learn body techniques, and their cultivation becomes body tempering.
- **Attribute resonance**: the ratio between your highest and lowest attribute (Combat / Spirit / Fortune / Physique) sets a live state shown on the character page — at ≤1.6, Four-Signs Resonance (cultivation +5%, with possible resonance encounters while exploring); at ≥2.5, Spiritual Imbalance (cultivation and exploration can trigger a mana surge, leaving a 6-month Spiritual Turmoil at cultivation −10%). Balanced attributes pay off.
- **Combat**: challenge local opponents on the d20 combat die; treasures, techniques and realm all fold into your strength.
- **Economy**: buy pills at the market, consign pills for spirit stones, build a spirit-gathering array at your cave dwelling. Note: spirit stones are wiped on death — don't hoard.
- **Social**: 12 NPCs can be befriended and visited, developing into five relationship types — ally, master, disciple, dao companion, enemy. Favor feeds social checks.

## Death & reincarnation

Dying of old age, falling in battle, a violent death, or severe qi deviation ends the life and opens **reincarnation settlement**:

- Reincarnation points are granted by the life's grade (S 6 / A 4 / B 3 / C 2), plus tiers for memory shards newly collected this life.
- Only one tenth of cultivation carries over. Memory is retained by manner of death (old age 30% / battle 10% / violent death 0%, with a realm bonus capped at 50%).
- Spend legacy points to redeem equipment and techniques you want to keep; pick one natal treasure — actually held this life and at its realm threshold — to carry over.
- Treasures you buried and karma you left unsettled wait for your next life.

The Reincarnation History page shows your reincarnation tree, past-life fragments and the chronicle of every life.

## Main storyline: The Past-Life Mystery

Collect 9 memory shards. At 1 / 3 / 5 / 7 / 9 shards, five chapters of your past-life identity are revealed; completing the set grants extra rewards and a past-life dao fruit to carry each life.

## Settings

- Three theme modes (light / dark / follow system), switched by the floating button at bottom-right.
- Saves export/import as JSON (format-compatible with the open-source edition — progress migrates both ways).
- AI connection settings live on the settings page (optional): enter your own API key or connect a local model; keys are stored only in your browser. The AI companion panel is on the roadmap; the game is fully playable without it.
